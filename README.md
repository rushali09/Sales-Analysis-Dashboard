# Super Store Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes Super Store sales performance across categories, sub-categories, customer segments, regions, states, ship modes, and payment methods. The goal of this project is to provide a clear business view of sales, profit, quantity sold, and delivery performance using interactive visuals and KPI cards.

The dashboard helps users quickly understand which products, regions, and customer groups are driving business performance.

## Dashboard Preview

The dashboard includes key business metrics such as:

- Total Sales: 1.57M
- Total Quantity Sold: 22K
- Total Profit: 175K
- Average Delivery Time: 4 days

It also includes visuals for sales by category, sub-category, ship mode, state, customer segment, region, payment segment, and monthly year-over-year sales and profit trends. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## Key Insights

### 1. Sales by Category

Office Supplies generated the highest sales at 0.64M, followed by Technology at 0.47M and Furniture at 0.45M.

### 2. Sales by Sub-Category

Phones, Chairs, and Binders were the top-performing sub-categories, contributing 0.20M, 0.18M, and 0.17M in sales respectively.

### 3. Sales by Ship Mode

Standard Class was the most used shipping method, contributing 0.33M in sales, followed by Second Class, First Class, and Same Day delivery.

### 4. Customer Segment Analysis

The Consumer segment contributed the highest share at 48%, followed by Corporate at 33% and Home Office at 19%.

### 5. Regional Performance

The West region had the highest contribution at 33%, followed by East at 29%, Central at 22%, and South at 16%.

### 6. Payment Method Analysis

Cash on Delivery was the most used payment method at 43%, followed by Online payments at 35% and Cards at 22%.

### 7. Monthly Sales and Profit Trends

The dashboard compares monthly sales and profit trends for 2019 and 2020, helping identify year-over-year performance changes and seasonal patterns.
